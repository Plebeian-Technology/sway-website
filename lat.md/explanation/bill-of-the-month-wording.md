# Bill of the Month wording and sourcing

Sway now features one bill a month, so all site copy says month, and the page that explains the choice was rewritten from the Sway application's own documentation.

## Month, not week

The Sway app renamed Bill of the Week to Bill of the Month and kept the old routes working, so the website follows the product name.

The old page moved to a new address, and a permanent redirect keeps old links and search results working. See [[reference/pages-and-redirect#Redirect]].

## Where the explanation comes from

The choosing page describes what the Sway repository records about how bills reach the feature slot, and keeps the old editorial criteria that the repository does not contradict.

From the Sway repository's lat.md folder it uses `bill-interesting-score.md` and `bill-interesting-score-design.md` for the five ranking signals and the roll-call exception, and `bill-creator-picker-mechanics.md` plus the admin schedule notes for the hand-scheduling by administrators. The diversity, cost, focus and impact criteria come from the old page. The repository says nothing for or against them.

## Known gaps

The app schema allows one featured bill per locale per day, and the site says "one bill a month".

The site copy states the editorial cadence, which the app does not enforce. The cadence gap was reported to the owner instead of edited. The privacy page was checked against the app separately; only claims the app contradicts were corrected.
